Safe profile for mixed, ambiguous, or not confidently classified images. NOT SENT TO ANY MODEL: images keyed to this profile always take the deterministic safe route, in every image mode.

What the safe route does (code: image/generation.py, _enhance_image_conservatively):
- automatic contrast stretch (autocontrast, 1% cutoff);
- slight contrast increase;
- slight colour saturation increase;
- slight sharpening.
Grayscale images get only the contrast stretch and the sharpening; transparency is kept.

What it does not do:
- it does not reinterpret, redraw, denoise, or upscale the image;
- it does not add or remove entities, labels, values, or relationships;
- it keeps the original layout, size, and format.

If the enhancement cannot be applied, the pipeline keeps the original image: it is the preserve-the-original fallback, never a generative step.

Do not turn this profile into a model prompt: the safe route must stay deterministic (spec 062 FR-019, Non-goals).
